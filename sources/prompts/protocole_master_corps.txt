IMAGE-EDITING TASK
REALISTIC PHOTOGRAPHIC BODY TRANSFORMATION
PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL PHOTOGRAPH

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Edit the supplied source photograph rather than generating a new photograph.

Transform the existing visible male physique into a naturally muscular, athletic and harmonious physique while preserving the original person, original photograph, original camera geometry, original pose, original environment and original photographic characteristics.

The result must look like the SAME photograph taken at the SAME moment, from the SAME camera position, with the SAME lighting and the SAME pose, except that the subject naturally possesses greater muscular development.

This is an anatomical volume modification, not a redesign of the person.

Do not recreate the photograph from scratch.
Do not reinterpret the scene.
Do not change the photographic perspective.
Do not change the camera position.
Do not change the pose unless a microscopic anatomical adjustment is strictly necessary to accommodate the increased muscular volume.
Do not beautify the photograph.
Do not apply a generic fitness-model appearance.
Do not apply a bodybuilding aesthetic.
Do not make the subject unnaturally lean or excessively muscular.

Never interpret a shadow as a missing anatomical structure.

Never invent anatomy simply because a muscular physique would normally contain it.

The farther structures must remain appropriately reduced.

==================================================
4. ANATOMICAL TRANSFORMATION
==================================================

Increase muscular development gradually and continuously rather than scaling the silhouette uniformly.

The target physique is:

- athletic
- naturally muscular
- proportionate
- visibly developed
- lean but not unnaturally shredded
- harmonious
- anatomically plausible
- compatible with a young adult male physiology

Avoid extreme bodybuilding proportions.

Avoid enormous deltoids.

Avoid oversized arms.

Avoid exaggerated abdominal definition.

Avoid artificial vascularity.

Avoid impossible muscle insertions.

- anterior deltoid
- lateral deltoid
- posterior deltoid
- clavicle
- upper pectoralis
- upper arm

No abrupt shoulder-to-arm transition.

==================================================
6. CHEST
==================================================

- upper pectoralis
- sternal pectoralis
- lower pectoral contour

Do not create a deep artificial central chest line.

Pectoral definition must remain dependent on the existing illumination.

- biceps brachii
- brachialis

- forearm musculature

The upper arm must have natural proximal-to-distal taper.

The elbow must remain anatomically stable.

The forearm must remain proportionate to the upper arm.

Preserve the original hand size.

Do not enlarge hands.

Do not alter finger anatomy.

Increase abdominal muscular development subtly.
